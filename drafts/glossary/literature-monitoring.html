Literature Monitoring

Literature monitoring is the ongoing review of scientific publications and other information sources to identify relevant safety or product-related data that may affect how an organization understands a product.

Illustration showing the concept in a life sciences context, with clear process flow and regulatory relevance.

Definition

Literature monitoring is the ongoing review of scientific publications and other information sources to identify relevant safety or product-related data that may affect how an organization understands a product.

Why it matters

The discipline matters because important information can appear in publications or scientific channels that are not captured by routine reporting mechanisms. Monitoring helps the organization remain aware of developments that may have safety, regulatory, or product strategy implications.

Real-world example

A company may identify a newly published study that changes the interpretation of an adverse event profile or raises a question about how a product is being used in practice.

Related regulations and frameworks

The practice is relevant to pharmacovigilance and is often considered within broader safety oversight frameworks and references from WHO, ICH, and other regulators.

Common misconceptions

It is sometimes treated as a peripheral task. In reality, it can be an important part of identifying information early and improving the quality of ongoing review.

How it relates to MedNova services

MedNova can support organizations that need a practical approach to gathering, reviewing, and organizing this kind of information within a broader safety or compliance framework.

Expanded frequently asked questions

What sources are monitored?

This may include journal articles, conference materials, and relevant scientific publications.

Why is it important?

Because safety-relevant information can emerge through scientific channels beyond formal adverse event reporting.

How does it fit into pharmacovigilance?

It helps create a more complete picture of the information environment around a product.

How does MedNova connect to it?

Through structured support for review and implementation of practical oversight processes.
