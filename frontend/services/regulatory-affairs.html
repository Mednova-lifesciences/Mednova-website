NAFDAC product registration

How a registration runs with us

For medicines, medical devices, food and nutraceuticals, and cosmetics. We start by confirming the right pathway, because the wrong classification is the costliest early mistake.

  1. Pathway and classification

    Confirm the product category and registration route, and list what the dossier needs.

  2. Dossier preparation

    Assemble technical, quality and labelling documents to NAFDAC's format and expectations.

  3. Readiness review

    Check the dossier for gaps and inconsistencies before it is filed.

  4. Submission and follow-up

    File, track progress and respond to NAFDAC queries.

  5. Lifecycle

    Renewals, variations and post-approval changes once the product is registered.

Regulatory intelligence

  • Practical recommendations and timelines

Connected work

Registration and safety, planned together

Registered products carry safety obligations from day one. Our pharmacovigilance team sets up QPPV cover and reporting alongside your registration.

Which products can you register?

Medicines, medical devices, food and nutraceutical products, and cosmetics regulated by NAFDAC.

Can you take over a registration already in progress?

Yes. We review what has been filed, fix gaps and handle follow-up with NAFDAC.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Our base is Nigeria. We advise on entry planning for other West African markets and can coordinate filings there.

Ready to register or renew?

Send us the product details and your target timeline, and we will outline the pathway.
