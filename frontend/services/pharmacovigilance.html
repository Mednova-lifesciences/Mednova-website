FAQ

Common questions about our pharmacovigilance services

What local safety reporting does MedNova support?

We support adverse event intake, case assessment, ICSR submission, signal review, and documentation for safety-related regulatory obligations.

How does MedNova help with QPPV readiness?

We help organisations document oversight responsibilities, establish review workflows, and prepare for the regulatory expectations of a Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance.

Can pharmacovigilance integrate with regulatory affairs?

Yes. We connect pharmacovigilance work with regulatory submissions, product registration, and lifecycle management so safety and compliance remain aligned.
