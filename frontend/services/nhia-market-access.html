NHIA market access

Build an NHIA submission on Nigerian evidence

We help manufacturers plan and prepare health technology assessment submissions against NHIA requirements, with local evidence and economic analysis suited to the Nigerian context.

For pharmaceutical and medical device teams seeking a local partner for HTA readiness, evidence generation, submission writing and appraisal support.

Discuss your NHIA case
A standard procedure

NHIA has a standard submission procedure for health technology assessment.

You choose who does the study

Manufacturers can contract any capable firm to conduct their HTA study.

Every report is appraised

Submissions need to address NHIA's reference case and process requirements. NHIA appraises submissions and makes its own coverage decision.

NHIA HTA service

Three ways to work with us

Start with a readiness review, then scope the evidence and analysis your product needs. Our health economist partner leads economic modelling; MedNova coordinates the local evidence work, submission writing and filing.

Package 1

HTA Readiness Check

The place every product starts.

  • Decision problem drafted: population, comparator, outcomes
  • Evidence and data gaps mapped against NHIA's reference case
  • A clear go or no-go call
  • Plan, timeline and quote for the full submission
Package 2

NHIA HTA Submission

For products with good published trial data and costs that can come from NHIA's own tariffs and price lists.

  • Systematic literature review
  • Cost-effectiveness model adapted to Nigeria
  • Budget impact model and sensitivity analyses
  • Full HTA report, filed with NHIA
  • Answers to NHIA's questions during appraisal
Package 3

Submission with local evidence

For products where NHIA will want Nigerian numbers, or published data is thin.

  • Everything in Package 2
  • Plus a Nigerian study to fill the gap: a hospital cost study, an EQ-5D-5L quality-of-life survey, or real-world data from patient records

Also available on top of any package: resubmission support, price and access scenarios, evidence plans for pipeline products, and training on NHIA HTA for your market access team.

How we help

How a submission runs

  1. HTA Readiness Check

    Is your product a strong candidate, and what evidence is missing?

  2. Evidence strategy

    What NHIA's reference case calls for, and a plan to fill every gap.

  3. Local evidence generation

    Nigerian quality-of-life, cost and real-world outcome studies.

  4. HTA study and submission

    Clinical summary, cost-effectiveness and budget impact analysis, written up and submitted through NHIA's procedure.

  5. Appraisal preparation

    Getting your report ready for NHIA's validity appraisal before its decision, with methods and data documented and reviewers' questions answered.

  6. Post-listing support

    Safety monitoring and real-world follow-up once your product is covered.
