About MedNova

A Lagos team for drug safety, registration and market access

MedNova Lifesciences works with pharmaceutical manufacturers, biotech companies, marketing authorisation holders, importers and clinical sponsors who need to meet regulatory and safety obligations in Nigeria and West Africa.

Mission

Clear, compliant and commercially sound

Our mission is to give life sciences companies clear, compliant and commercially sound support that protects patients and keeps products moving.

We want to be the partner companies call first when a product has to be registered, monitored or covered in Nigeria, because the work is done properly and on time.

Leadership

Dr Seun Alabi, Founder and CEO

A physician whose career has been in pharmacovigilance and drug safety at global pharmaceutical companies and CROs. Seun founded MedNova to bring that standard of safety and regulatory work to companies operating in Nigeria and West Africa.

How we work

What clients can expect

Senior people on your work

You deal directly with experienced practitioners, not a rotating account team.

Safety and regulatory in one place

Pharmacovigilance, registration and market access are planned together so nothing falls between vendors.

Flexible engagement

Inspection-ready records
