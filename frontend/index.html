Why MedNova

Local NHIA evidence, connected to product safety and regulation

Health economics firms can model value; market-access advisers can shape launch strategy. MedNova brings those capabilities into the Nigerian product lifecycle: NAFDAC registration, pharmacovigilance and NHIA submission support coordinated by one Lagos-based team.

For HTA submissions, our health economist partner leads the economic models while MedNova coordinates evidence planning, local study support, writing and filing. This gives manufacturers a practical local interface without implying that MedNova is the decision-maker: NHIA independently appraises submissions and makes coverage decisions.
