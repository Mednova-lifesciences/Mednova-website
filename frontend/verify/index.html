Important

What a verification result will and will not tell you

MedNova Verify will not describe a medicine as “genuine” on the strength of a registration record alone. Matching a regulatory record and authenticating a physical pack are different things, and the platform reports them separately.

Registration Record Found
Matching registration information exists for the product details supplied. This does not by itself authenticate the individual physical pack in your hand.
No Matching Record Found
No registration record matched the details supplied. This may indicate an unregistered product, or a data entry problem.
Unable to Verify
The check could not be completed — for example because a data source was unavailable at the time of the request.
Product Information Mismatch
A record was found, but one or more details on the pack differ from the registration record. Worth reporting.
Pack Authentication Confirmed
Returned only where an authorised pack-authentication mechanism has confirmed that specific pack.
