Glossary Entry

PSUR

A PSUR is a periodic safety update report that summarizes the safety information available for a product during a defined reporting period.

Definition

The Periodic Safety Update Report, or PSUR, is a structured document used to summarize important safety information about a product over a defined reporting interval. It is designed to support the review of cumulative experience and inform ongoing benefit-risk assessment.

PSURs are commonly used in regulated environments where safety oversight must be documented and updated over time. They are particularly relevant where products are already marketed and new data continues to emerge.

Why It Matters

PSURs help organizations monitor the evolving safety profile of a product and respond appropriately to new information. They are an important bridge between routine safety operations and periodic regulatory reporting expectations.

They relate closely to aggregate reporting, PBRER, and post-marketing surveillance.

How It Works

A PSUR typically includes a summary of new safety findings, an update of the benefit-risk assessment, and actions taken or proposed in response to emerging evidence. It may also describe the data sources and methods used to assess safety information during the reporting period.

Because it is periodic, the PSUR provides a regular review point to ensure safety oversight remains aligned with the current profile of the product.

Regulatory Purpose

Regulators use PSURs to verify that companies are maintaining an active view of product safety. A well-prepared PSUR demonstrates that safety signals are being monitored, evaluated, and managed in a way that supports patient safety and compliance.

It also helps regulators decide whether additional risk minimization measures, changes to product information, or further studies are needed.
