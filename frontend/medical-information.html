Reliable Scientific Communication

MedNova helps life sciences organizations establish structured Medical Information processes that improve communication, maintain scientific accuracy, and support regulatory compliance throughout the product lifecycle.

We also help clients connect Medical Information with pharmacovigilance readiness and NAFDAC expectations through the NAFDAC QPPV Compliance Checklist, the downloadable checklist PDF, and glossary guidance for adverse drug reaction reporting and Good Pharmacovigilance Practice.

Our service model supports consistent response handling and clear scientific exchange, helping teams maintain credibility and confidence in the information they provide to external and internal stakeholders.

FAQ

Common questions about Medical Information

What is Medical Information?

Medical Information is the function responsible for answering scientific and product-related questions from healthcare professionals, patients, and internal stakeholders.

Who uses Medical Information services?

These services are used by pharmaceutical companies, biotech organizations, medical device manufacturers, healthcare institutions, and CROs.

Can MedNova support healthcare professionals?

Yes. We help organizations provide timely, evidence-based responses that support healthcare professionals and improve stakeholder experience.

Do you provide scientific response support?

Yes. MedNova supports structured scientific response development with review, consistency, and quality control.

Can Medical Information integrate with Pharmacovigilance?

Yes. Medical Information processes often coordinate closely with pharmacovigilance and other functions to ensure consistency and appropriate escalation.
