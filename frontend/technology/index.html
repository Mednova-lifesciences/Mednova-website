Technology

Tools that make safety work faster and more consistent

We build our own tools for the checking and review work in pharmacovigilance. They support our people; they do not replace them.

Available now

PV Assist

PV Assist is MedNova's pharmacovigilance review platform, used by our safety team on client work. It takes on repetitive checks so reviewers can spend their time on medical and scientific judgement.

Every output is reviewed by a qualified person before it is used or submitted.

Request a PV Assist demo
  • Line-listing quality checks and clean-up
  • Seriousness detection
  • MedDRA and WHODrug coding suggestions
  • Signal screening
  • E2B(R3) file preparation and review
  • PSUR section review
Coming soon

MedNova Verify

A medicine verification service for Nigeria. Patients, pharmacies and hospitals will be able to check a product's NAFDAC registration record and report safety, quality or falsification concerns.

Verify is still in development and is not open to the public yet. Contact us if you would like early access as a manufacturer, pharmacy or hospital.
