# MedNova SEO Audit Report

## Baseline findings before implementation

- Core pages already had titles, meta descriptions, canonical tags, Open Graph tags, Twitter cards, and schema markup on the main service pages.
- The site had strong structural foundations for accessibility and navigation, including semantic main content and visible heading hierarchy on key pages.
- The highest-impact opportunities were around keyword targeting for the pharmacovigilance and QPPV content, stronger relevance on supporting service pages, and reducing duplicate-content risk from the source template directory.
- Technical SEO could be strengthened by tightening robots directives and refreshing the sitemap metadata.

## Planned implementation

- Improve metadata relevance and uniqueness across core service pages.
- Strengthen the pharmacovigilance page content for searches such as pharmacovigilance Nigeria, drug safety services Nigeria, signal detection, literature monitoring, ICSR processing, and risk management.
- Add FAQ structured data to the pharmacovigilance page.
- Update robots instructions to prevent indexing of template or duplicate source pages.
- Refresh the sitemap metadata and keep the canonical structure aligned to the live URLs.
